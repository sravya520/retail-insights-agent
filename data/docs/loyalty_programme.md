# Loyalty Programme

Document code LP-05. Version 1.0. Effective 1 January 2024. This is a synthetic policy for the fictional company Northwind Gifts. It is used only for a software demonstration.

## 1. Joining

**1.1 Free membership.** Membership of the Loyalty Programme is free for retail customers with a Northwind Gifts account.

**1.2 Who can join.** Wholesale accounts cannot join. Members must be aged 16 or over.

**1.3 Welcome bonus.** A new member receives 200 points after the first order is delivered.

## 2. Earning points

**2.1 Base rate.** A member earns 1 point for every £1 spent on goods. Delivery charges, VAT and gift cards do not earn points. Points are calculated after discounts and vouchers.

**2.2 Tier multipliers.** Silver members earn 1.25 points per £1. Gold members earn 1.5 points per £1.

**2.3 Birthday bonus.** A member receives 100 bonus points in the month of their birthday.

**2.4 Referral bonus.** When a member refers a friend, both receive 250 points after the friend's first order of at least £20 is delivered.

**2.5 Review points.** A member earns 20 points for each product review, up to 5 reviews per month.

**2.6 When points appear.** Points are added to the account 30 days after the order is delivered. This gives time for returns.

## 3. Tiers

**3.1 Tier thresholds.** Every member starts at Bronze. A member reaches Silver after earning 1,000 points in the last 12 months. A member reaches Gold after earning 3,000 points in the last 12 months.

**3.2 Silver benefits.** Silver members get free UK standard delivery on orders of £25 or more, instead of £40.

**3.3 Gold benefits.** Gold members get free UK standard delivery on all orders, and free UK next day delivery on orders of £60 or more.

**3.4 Tier review.** Tiers are checked each month using the last 12 months of earned points. A member who falls below a threshold moves down one tier.

## 4. Redeeming points

**4.1 Redemption rate.** 500 points can be exchanged for a £5 voucher. Points can only be redeemed in steps of 500.

**4.2 Voucher validity.** A voucher is valid for 90 days from the day it is issued.

**4.3 Voucher limit.** A voucher can pay for at most 50 percent of the basket value.

**4.4 Excluded items.** Vouchers cannot be used on gift cards or on Final Sale items.

**4.5 No cash value.** Points and vouchers have no cash value and cannot be exchanged for money.

## 5. Expiry

**5.1 Inactivity expiry.** All points expire if the account has no earning or redeeming activity for 12 months.

**5.2 Reminder.** Northwind Gifts sends a reminder email 30 days before points expire.

**5.3 Account closure.** If a member closes their account, all points and unused vouchers are lost.

## 6. Returns and points

**6.1 Points on refunds.** When an item is refunded, the points earned on it are removed from the balance.

**6.2 Negative balance.** If the points were already redeemed, the balance may become negative. The next points earned are used to clear the negative balance.

**6.3 Vouchers on returned orders.** If a voucher was used on an order that is fully refunded, the voucher value is returned as store credit and not as a new voucher.

## 7. Changes and misuse

**7.1 Changes to the programme.** Northwind Gifts may change the rules with 30 days notice by email.

**7.2 Misuse.** Fraud or misuse leads to removal of points and closure of the account.

**7.3 No transfers.** Points cannot be transferred between accounts or sold.
