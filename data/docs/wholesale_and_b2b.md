# Wholesale and B2B Terms

Document code WB-04. Version 1.0. Effective 1 January 2024. This is a synthetic policy for the fictional company Northwind Gifts. It is used only for a software demonstration.

## 1. Eligibility

**1.1 Who qualifies.** A wholesale account is open to registered businesses that give a company registration number and a VAT number. Sole traders may apply with proof of trading, such as a tax registration letter.

**1.2 Application time.** Northwind Gifts reviews wholesale applications within 5 business days.

**1.3 Retail customers.** Retail customers cannot use wholesale prices. Wholesale prices are shown only after the account is approved.

## 2. Minimum orders

**2.1 First order minimum.** The first wholesale order must be at least £250 before VAT.

**2.2 Reorder minimum.** Every later wholesale order must be at least £150 before VAT.

**2.3 Carton sizes.** Most products are sold in cartons of 6 or 12 units. Single units cannot be ordered on a wholesale account.

**2.4 Orders below the minimum.** Orders below the minimum are not processed. The customer is told by email.

## 3. Pricing and discounts

**3.1 Volume discounts.** A discount is applied to each order based on the order value before VAT. Orders of £500 to £1,999 receive 5 percent. Orders of £2,000 to £4,999 receive 8 percent. Orders of £5,000 or more receive 12 percent.

**3.2 Promotions.** Volume discounts cannot be combined with retail promotions or vouchers.

**3.3 Price protection.** The price on a confirmed wholesale order is fixed for 90 days.

**3.4 VAT.** All wholesale prices are shown before VAT. VAT is added at the standard rate on the invoice.

## 4. Payment and credit

**4.1 Prepayment for new accounts.** A new account must pay in advance for its first 3 orders.

**4.2 Credit approval.** After 3 paid orders and a successful credit check, the customer may apply for credit terms.

**4.3 Standard credit terms.** Standard credit terms are payment within 30 days of the invoice date. The starting credit limit is £2,000.

**4.4 Credit limit review.** The credit limit is reviewed every 6 months. A higher limit can be agreed if the account has no late payments.

**4.5 Late payment interest.** Interest of 1.5 percent per month is charged on overdue invoices.

**4.6 Account suspension.** An account is suspended when any invoice is more than 15 days overdue. New orders are not accepted while the account is suspended.

**4.7 Early payment discount.** A discount of 2 percent is given when an invoice is paid within 10 days.

## 5. Orders and delivery

**5.1 Lead time.** Wholesale orders are dispatched within 3 business days of a confirmed order, or paid order for accounts without credit terms.

**5.2 Delivery charges.** Wholesale delivery charges are set out in the Shipping and Delivery policy.

**5.3 Cancellations.** A wholesale order can be cancelled free of charge before dispatch if it is under £1,000. For orders of £1,000 or more, a cancellation fee of 10 percent of the order value applies.

## 6. Returns and damages

**6.1 Return window.** Wholesale returns for change of mind must be made within 14 days of delivery and carry a 15 percent restocking fee, as set out in the Returns and Refunds policy.

**6.2 Reporting damage.** Damage and shortages must be reported within 48 hours of delivery.

**6.3 Faulty goods.** Faulty goods are covered by a 6 month warranty. See the Warranty and Product Quality policy.

## 7. Loyalty and promotions

**7.1 No loyalty points.** Wholesale accounts do not earn or redeem loyalty points.

**7.2 Retail vouchers.** Retail vouchers and promotion codes cannot be used on wholesale orders.

## 8. Ending an account

**8.1 Notice.** Either side may end the account with 30 days written notice. Open invoices must be paid in full.

**8.2 Immediate closure.** Northwind Gifts may close an account at once if an invoice is more than 60 days overdue.
