# Customer Data and Privacy

Document code DP-06. Version 1.0. Effective 1 January 2024. This is a synthetic policy for the fictional company Northwind Gifts. It is used only for a software demonstration.

## 1. Who we are

**1.1 Data controller.** Northwind Gifts Ltd is the controller of the personal data described in this policy.

**1.2 Contact.** Privacy questions can be sent to the data protection officer at privacy@northwindgifts.example.

## 2. What we collect

**2.1 Account and order data.** We collect name, email address, delivery address, phone number and order history.

**2.2 Payment data.** Card numbers are handled by our payment processor. Northwind Gifts does not store full card numbers or security codes.

**2.3 Device and cookie data.** We collect device type, pages viewed and cookie identifiers when a customer uses the website.

**2.4 Call recordings.** Customer service calls are recorded for training and quality. Recordings are deleted after 90 days.

## 3. Why we use data

**3.1 To deliver orders.** We use account and order data to take payment, deliver orders and handle returns.

**3.2 Legal duties.** We keep tax and accounting records because the law requires it.

**3.3 Marketing with consent.** We send marketing emails only to customers who have given consent.

**3.4 Fraud prevention.** We use order and device data to detect and prevent fraud. This is a legitimate interest.

## 4. Retention

**4.1 Order and invoice records.** Order and invoice records are kept for 7 years because of tax law.

**4.2 Marketing consent.** Marketing consent records are kept until the customer withdraws consent, or for 24 months after the last activity on the account, whichever comes first.

**4.3 Inactive accounts.** Accounts with no login or order for 36 months are deleted.

**4.4 Support tickets.** Customer service tickets are kept for 3 years.

**4.5 Wholesale records.** Wholesale account records are kept for 7 years after the account is closed.

## 5. Customer rights

**5.1 Access requests.** A customer can ask for a copy of their personal data. Northwind Gifts replies within 30 days.

**5.2 Erasure.** A customer can ask for their data to be deleted. Northwind Gifts completes the deletion within 30 days, except for data that must be kept by law.

**5.3 Correction.** A customer can ask for wrong data to be corrected. Corrections are made within 30 days.

**5.4 Stopping marketing.** A customer can opt out of marketing at any time. Northwind Gifts stops marketing emails within 48 hours.

**5.5 Data portability.** A customer can ask for their order history as a CSV file.

**5.6 Identity checks.** Northwind Gifts may ask for proof of identity before acting on a rights request.

## 6. Sharing data

**6.1 Couriers.** We share name, address and phone number with couriers to deliver parcels.

**6.2 Payment processor.** We share payment details with our payment processor.

**6.3 No sale of data.** Northwind Gifts does not sell personal data.

**6.4 International transfers.** If data is sent outside the UK, we use approved safeguards such as standard contract clauses.

## 7. Cookies

**7.1 Essential cookies.** Essential cookies are always on. They are needed for the basket and login.

**7.2 Optional cookies.** Analytics and marketing cookies are used only if the customer gives consent.

**7.3 Cookie consent expiry.** Cookie consent is asked again after 12 months.

## 8. Children

**8.1 Account age.** Customers under 16 cannot create an account without a parent or guardian.

**8.2 Marketing to minors.** Northwind Gifts does not send marketing emails to anyone under 18.

## 9. Security and breaches

**9.1 Security measures.** Personal data is encrypted in transit and at rest.

**9.2 Staff access.** Staff can see personal data only if they need it for their job.

**9.3 Reporting a breach to the regulator.** If a personal data breach puts people at risk, Northwind Gifts reports it to the Information Commissioner's Office within 72 hours.

**9.4 Telling customers.** If the breach is likely to cause a high risk to customers, Northwind Gifts tells them without undue delay.

## 10. Links to other policies

**10.1 Erasure and invoices.** If a customer asks for erasure, order and invoice records are kept for the period in clause 4.1. Other data is deleted.

**10.2 Erasure and loyalty points.** Closing an account or asking for erasure removes the customer's loyalty points, as set out in the Loyalty Programme policy.
